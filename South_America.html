    • 🌎 South America

    Learn about the geography, countries, and features of South America.

    Facts

    • South America, fourth largest of the world’s continents.
    • Southern portion of the Americas, known for the Andes and Amazon.
    • Geography & Peaks: Mount Aconcagua in Argentina is the tallest mountain in both the Southern and Western Hemispheres at 6,961 meters.
    • South America has a total area of about 6,878,000 square miles (17,814,000 square km), or roughly one-eighth of the land surface of Earth.

    Major Countries

    Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Peru, and etc.

    What are the Continents?

    There are seven continents in the world: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia/Oceania, Europe, North America, and South America. However, depending on where you live, you may have learned that there are five, six, or even four continents. This is because there is no official criteria for determining continents. While the position of landmasses on continental crust may be used to determine continents, geopolitical factors also affect their delineation. Below is an overview of the world's continent using the most popular classification system, the seven continent method.

    North America

    North America (24.7 millionsquare kilometre): Includes 23 countries, bordered by the Arctic, Atlantic, and Pacific Oceans.

    Fact: North America contains many climate zones, from Arctic cold to tropical heat.

    South America

    South America (17.8 million square kilometre): Contains the Andes mountains and the Amazon basin.

    Fact: South America is home to the Amazon Rainforest, the largest rainforest on Earth.

    Africa

    Africa (30.4 million square kilometre): Second largest, known for vast deserts and tropical regions.

    Fact: Africa is the second-largest continent and is home to the world’s longest river, the Nile.

    Europe

    Europe (10.2 million square kilometre): Characterized by a high population density and distinct cultural regions.

    Fact: Europe is known for its many countries, cultures, and long history.

    Antarctica

    Antarctica (14.0 million square kilometre): The coldest, southernmost continent, with no permanent population.

    Fact: Antarctica is the coldest continent and has no permanent human population.

    Aisa

    Asia (44.6 million square kilometre): Largest and most populous, containing the Himalayas and many diverse climates.

    Fact: Asia is the largest continent and has the highest population in the world.

    Australia

    Australia (8.6 million square kilometre): Smallest continent, often referred to as part of the Oceania region.

    Fact: Australia is the smallest continent and is surrounded by oceans and seas.

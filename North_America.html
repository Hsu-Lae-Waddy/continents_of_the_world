    • 🌎 North America

    Learn about the geography, countries, and features of North America.

    Facts

    • North America is the third-largest continent in the world, after Asia and Africa. It covers about 24.7 million square kilometres, which is nearly 16.5% of Earth’s total land area.
    • The Missouri River is the longest one in North America, stretching approximately 3,767 kilometres.
    • Greenland, the world’s largest island, lies in North America and is governed by Denmark.
    • It has diverse climates from Arctic to tropical.

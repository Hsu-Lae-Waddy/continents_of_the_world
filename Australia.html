    • 🌎 Australia

    Learn about the geography, countries, and features of Australia.

    Facts

    • Smallest continent, often referred to as Oceania or Australasia.
    • Australia holds the oldest continental crust on Earth, researchers have confirmed, hills some 4.4 billion years old.
    • The animals of Australia are some of the most interesting in the world, from cuddly marsupials to a huge variety of birds.

    Major Countries

    Australia, Papua New Guinea, New Zealand, Fiji, etc.

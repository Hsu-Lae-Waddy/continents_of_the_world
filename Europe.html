    • 🌎 Europe

    Learn about the geography, countries, and features of Europe.

    Facts

    • Peninsula-like continent, often defined by culture and geography.
    • Europe is the world's second-smallest continent by surface area, covering about 2% of Earth's surface, yet it is a major, densely populated cultural hub with roughly 50 sovereign states and over 200 languages.
    • Europe's wealth of attractions is its biggest draw: the birthplace of democracy in Athens, the Renaissance art in Florence, the graceful canals of Venice, the Napoleonic splendor of Paris, the impressive attractions of Moorish palaces in Andalucia.
    • It is surrounded by the Arctic Ocean to the north, the Atlantic Ocean to the west, Asia to the east and Africa to the south.

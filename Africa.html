    • 🌎 Africa

    Learn about the geography, countries, and features of Africa.

    Facts

    • Second largest and second most populous, known for diverse cultures and geographic features.
    • The Sahara is the world's largest hot desert.
    • The Nile is the longest river in the world. Lake Victoria is Africa's largest lake and the world's second-largest freshwater lake.
    • It produces 70% of the world's cocoa beans

    Major Countries

    Algeria (largest), DR Congo, Sudan, Libya, Chad , etc.

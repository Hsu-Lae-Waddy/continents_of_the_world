    • 🌎 Antarctica

    Learn about the geography, countries, and features of Antarctica.

    Facts

    • Southernmost, uninhabited (except for research), and fifth largest continent.
    • Antarctica is the coldest, windiest and most remote continent on Earth.
    • It is also the world’s highest, driest, windiest, coldest, and iciest continent.

    Major Countries

    There are no countries in Antarctica. Seven countries made defined claims to Antarctic territory prior to the Antarctic Treaty of 1959. The treaty does not legally recognize any claims.
